Our story

About Harnith Collection

Harnith Collection started with a simple frustration: good everyday basics — sweatpants that actually fit, office trousers that hold a crease, a collar shirt that works both ways — shouldn't be hard to find or priced like they're special-occasion pieces. So we built a small catalog around exactly that: pieces for daily rotation, for men and women, priced like they're still on the hanger.

What we carry

Sweatpants, office trousers, collar shirts and sneakers form the core of the collection, in both men's and women's cuts. Alongside that runs our thrift line — hand-picked, quality-checked secondhand pieces. Every thrift item is one-of-one, so once it's gone, that exact piece isn't coming back in the same size or condition.

How we work

We're a small, growing operation. Orders are reviewed and fulfilled by hand, not by a warehouse — which means real attention to each order, and occasionally a little more patience than a big retailer would ask of you. If anything about your order needs a human, reach out and we'll sort it directly.
