Delivery

We currently ship within Nigeria only. Orders are packed and handed to a delivery partner within 2–4 business days of payment confirmation. Delivery time after dispatch depends on your location — typically 1–2 days within Lagos, and 3–5 days for other states.

Delivery fee

A flat delivery fee applies at checkout, shown before you pay. This may change to a location-based rate as we grow.

Store pickup

Choose "Store pickup" at checkout to collect your order in person at no extra cost. We'll confirm a pickup location and time once your order is placed.

Order tracking

We don't yet have automated tracking numbers — you'll be updated directly by the contact details you provide at checkout. If you haven't heard from us within the expected window, reach out.
