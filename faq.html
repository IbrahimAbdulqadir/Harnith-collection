Questions

Frequently asked

How do I know what size to order?

Each product page lists the sizes we currently have in stock for that piece. Sweatpants and shirts run true to standard S–XXL sizing; trousers and sneakers are listed in numeric sizes. If you're between sizes or unsure, message us before ordering and we'll help you pick.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card, bank transfer and USSD via Paystack. Payment is processed securely at checkout — we never see or store your card details.

How long does delivery take?

See our Shipping page for full details — as a rule of thumb, orders ship within 2–4 business days of payment confirmation.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Most items can be returned within our return window — see the Returns page for the full policy. Thrift pieces are one-of-one and sold as-is, so please check the listing carefully before ordering.

What does "flagship" mean on a product?

Flagship pieces are the ones we're currently leading with — usually our best sellers or newest additions. It's just a way of surfacing them first when you browse.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Not yet — right now we fulfill within Nigeria only. If that changes, this page (and the shipping page) will be updated.

I have a question that's not answered here.

Reach out through the contact page and we'll get back to you directly.
