What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect what you give us directly: name, email, phone number, and delivery address if you choose delivery. We don't collect payment card details — those go straight to our payment processor, Paystack.

Your cart and wishlist

Your cart and saved items are stored only in your browser (via localStorage), not on a server. Clearing your browser data clears them, and they don't follow you to another device.

How we use your data

Order and contact details are used only to fulfill your order, communicate with you about it, and respond to your enquiries. We don't sell your data.

Third parties

Order and contact form submissions are processed through Netlify Forms and delivered to us by email. Payments are processed by Paystack. Both providers handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We keep order and enquiry records for as long as reasonably needed for accounting, customer service and legal purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site and business evolve. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
