Return window

Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, unworn, unwashed, with tags attached and in their original condition.

Thrift items

Thrift pieces are one-of-one, sold as-is, and described as accurately as possible at listing. Because each piece is unique and secondhand, thrift items are final sale unless the item received doesn't match its listing.

How to start a return

Contact us through the contact page with your order details and reason for return. We'll confirm eligibility and next steps directly.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method once the returned item is received and checked. Please allow a few business days for the refund to reflect, depending on your bank.

Exchanges

Need a different size instead? Let us know when you start your return and we'll arrange an exchange where stock allows.
